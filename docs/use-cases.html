InboxGenius: use cases and user stories

InboxGenius reads the shared interview inbox, labels every email, and gives staff a dashboard to check the work. The newest part tells each student about their interview by call, text and email, and keeps following up until they confirm. Status as of 11 Oct 2026, after the deploy of commit b525ca7. Production runs the student notifications in shadow mode: the app does everything except deliver, so no student has been contacted yet. Roles replace people: the approver, the callback owner and the escalation contact are set on the dashboard Settings page (today: Kes for the rehearsal, Rob, Jackie).

11working 5partial 5in shadow mode or off The student-notification feature (UC12 to UC17) has run in shadow mode on the server since 9 Oct: it finds the real GHL contact and logs what it would send. The three GHL write switches stay off until Kes has checked that log; the staff test with three Colaberry staff is planned for Mon 12 Oct. The voice call itself was proven on a live test call on 2 Oct; text, email, voicemail, wrong-person and STOP tests are still to do (see the test plan).
