InboxGenius: use cases and user stories

InboxGenius reads the shared interview inbox, labels every email, and gives staff a dashboard to check the work. The newest part tells each student about their interview by call, text and email, and keeps following up until they confirm. Status as of 2 Oct 2026, after the deploy of commit 2298382.

7working 5partial 5built, switched off The student-notification feature (UC12 to UC17) is built and deployed but off: no credentials on the server, every channel is a dry run. The voice call itself was proven on a live test call on 2 Oct; text, email, voicemail, wrong-person and STOP tests are still to do (see the test plan).
